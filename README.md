# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

## Research Objective

This repository contains research for building a practical LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy playbook for B2B SaaS companies.

The goal is to understand how leading practitioners use LinkedIn to:

- Build audience and authority
- Generate awareness and demand
- Create trust through content
- Grow personal and company brands
- Influence pipeline and revenue

Rather than relying on marketing theory, this research focuses on experts who actively publish content, build audiences, generate business results, and publicly share their strategies.

---

## Research Question

**How do B2B SaaS companies use LinkedIn organic content to generate awareness, engagement, trust, and pipeline?**

---

## Expert Selection Criteria

Experts were selected based on the following criteria:

- Actively publish content on LinkedIn
- Have proven audience growth and business outcomes
- Share practical frameworks rather than generic advice
- Represent different aspects of LinkedIn growth and demand generation
- Produce recent content (2025–2026)

The research includes LinkedIn creators, marketers, consultants, founders, podcast guests, and YouTube creators who actively practice what they teach.
