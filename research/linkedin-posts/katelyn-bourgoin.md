## Post 2

**Date:** September 13, 2025

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/katebour_what-do-codie-sanchez-gary-v-and-simon-activity-7364625379589820416-qcKR

**Summary:**  
Bourgoin analyzes what Codie Sanchez, Gary Vaynerchuk, and Simon Sinek share in common: each built explosive growth around an "ownable idea"—a sticky, revelatory concept synonymous with the creator ("Start With Why," "Buy boring businesses," "Document, Don't Create"). After studying thousands of B2B thought leaders, she distills six criteria for an ownable idea: **Revelatory** (reframes how people see the world), **Directive** (tells people what to do or believe), **Sticky** (simple and repeatable), **Aspirational** (offers a future identity), **Credible** (backed by proof or lived expertise), and **Buyable** (ties directly to revenue). She teases a roadmap and domain purchase (ownableideas.com) as she builds out the framework publicly on LinkedIn.

**Key Takeaway:**  
Message-market fit for B2B SaaS organic content starts with one ownable idea that passes six tests—not a content calendar of disconnected tips; the idea becomes the content engine, positioning anchor, and demand-generation asset.

## Post 3

**Date:** June 13, 2025

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/katebour_many-marketers-confuse-listening-to-their-activity-7292919436464848897-C16Q

**Summary:**  
Bourgoin draws a sharp line between asking customers what they *think* they'd do and observing what they *actually* did—a common VoC research mistake. She argues that golden insights live in buying stories, best extracted through 1:1 interviews she calls **Clarity Calls**, which she runs before investing in major marketing campaigns or designing new offers. She claims 30-minute calls produce "mind boggling" aha moments and teases that three Clarity Calls scheduled that week signal an upcoming product or campaign launch—modeling how customer research directly feeds content and offer development.

**Key Takeaway:**  
B2B SaaS content ideation should start with Clarity Calls (past-behavior interviews, not hypothetical surveys) to extract trigger events and buying stories—VoC research is the upstream input for LinkedIn posts, messaging, and campaign design.

## Research Notes

Recurring Themes:
- Buyer psychology over algorithm hacks ("whoever gets closer to the customer wins")
- VoC research as content strategy input, not a separate research phase
- Clarity Calls: 1:1 interviews focused on past buying behavior, not hypothetical preferences
- Trigger Technique: trigger event → job-to-be-done → pains with alternatives → selfish desires → trigger tests
- Painkiller vs. vitamin messaging ("when you find their pains you find your profits")
- Pre-suasion and priming before pitches (Robert Cialdini framework applied to launches)
- Ownable ideas as message-market fit mechanism for expert and B2B brands
- Content evolution through audience feedback ("clarity comes from engagement—not thought")
- Study human behavior, not algorithms—psychology shifts at glacial speed
- Input goals over growth goals (show up consistently; first 100 posts whisper, 101st roars)
