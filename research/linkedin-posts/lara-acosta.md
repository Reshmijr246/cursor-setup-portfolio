## Research Notes

Recurring Themes:
- Personal brand as business infrastructure, not vanity metrics ("authority at scale—clients choose you because of you")
- Proof-first content: case studies, screenshots, before→after, stage photos—not generic tips
- SLAY Framework: Story (hook) → Lesson (insight) → Actionable advice (lists/steps) → You (reader-directed question/CTA)
- Expert-preneur positioning: depth and expertise over dopamine/volume as LinkedIn reverts to expert-led feeds
- Content for attention, email for conversion—strict sequencing before funnel/webinar monetization
- Vertical stacking: one validated niche → agency → info product → software (same audience, same problem, more leverage)
- Niche validation via data, not passion ("I didn't decide my niche—the data told me")
- Build in public / document experiments vs. only educating as finished expert
- Community reciprocity: strategic collaboration, public support, comment protocols (30 min before/after posting)
- Owned audience hedge: newsletter and email list as conversion layer atop LinkedIn attention
- Signature content style + topic discipline for algorithm memorability
- Rejection of pods, paid engagement rings, and algorithm hack chasing
- Creator energy and fun as competitive moat in B2B content
