## Post 3

**Date:** May 24, 2026

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/richardvanderblom_59-linkedin-formats-dos-and-donts-chapter-activity-7464297770414133248-T9K2

**Summary:**  
Chapter 3 free insights tied to LinkedIn’s new AI moderation crackdown on pods, automated comments, and engagement-bait CTAs (“comment X and I’ll DM you”). Richard notes thousands of posts were mistakenly removed, but the direction is clear: formats that reward depth and intent win. Documents now outperform other formats on reach and engagement; polls travel far but generate less conversation; video has fallen below average for the first time.

**Key Takeaway:**  
B2B SaaS teams should rebalance format mix toward save-worthy documents and depth-driven content, and avoid engagement-bait tactics that LinkedIn is actively penalizing.
