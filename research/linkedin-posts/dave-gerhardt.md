# Dave Gerhardt

**Category:** Founder Brand & Community-Led Growth

**LinkedIn Profile:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/davegerhardt/

**Why Selected:**  
Former VP of Marketing at Drift (employee #8; helped create the Conversational Marketing category and grow from $0 to eight-figure revenue) and CMO at Privy (repositioned the company and supported its nine-figure acquisition by Attentive). Author of *Founder Brand* (2022), which codifies founder-led storytelling as a B2B competitive advantage. Built Exit Five from a Patreon side project into a bootstrapped, profitable B2B media company—6,000+ member community, podcast, newsletter, and in-person events—with LinkedIn organic content as the primary growth engine (~200K followers; community sources report LinkedIn drives ~60% of signups). His playbook—treat LinkedIn as the #1 PR channel, compound weekly through writing, evolve beyond the founder's name into a standalone brand—directly maps to B2B SaaS organic strategy, executive activation, and brand-driven demand generation.

## Post 1

**Date:** May 30, 2026

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/davegerhardt_the-narrative-is-shifting-on-ai-have-you-activity-7464265192814706688-TOLZ

**Summary:**  
Gerhardt shares a community-sourced observation—not a hot take—that B2B marketers in his Exit Five network are pushing back harder on AI hype than they were 60 days prior. He contrasts genuine AI utility (e.g., never logging into ads managers again) with fear-inducing headlines like "I replaced our VP of Marketing with AI," and asks the audience why sentiment is shifting and what happens next. The post generated 240+ comments, with marketers debating job security, keynote booing, and whether AI positioning should lead with capability vs. problem-solving.

**Key Takeaway:**  
Observation-style posts grounded in community intelligence build trust and engagement without prescribing a POV; B2B SaaS founders can use their audience as a research panel to surface category shifts before competitors catch on.

- Personal brand → media company → standalone community brand (DGMG → Exit Five evolution)
- Community intelligence as content source (Exit Five member conversations inform observations)

- Separating founder role from operator role (Dan Murphy promoted to CEO, Dec 2025)

Content & Business Model Context:
- Gerhardt launched Exit Five from a Patreon subscription ("The A List") after building LinkedIn and email audience while CMO at Privy; reached 1,000 paying members at $10/month within six months ([LinkedIn article: *From PR Intern to CMO to One-Person Business*](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-pr-intern-cmo-one-person-business-dave-gerhardt)).

- Exit Five LinkedIn strategy reportedly drives ~60% of community signups; team maintains ~2M+ monthly impressions across multiple accounts ([Community Inc. deep dive](https://community.inc/article/exit-five)).
- *Founder Brand* (Lioncrest Publishing, 2022) remains the canonical framework for executive-led LinkedIn programs in B2B SaaS.

Additional Context (2025–2026):
- Dan Murphy promoted from COO to CEO of Exit Five effective December 1, 2025, freeing Gerhardt to focus on content, storytelling, and vision ([Exit Five Podcast #311](https://www.exitfive.com/podcast/five-marketing-principles-that-matter-more-than-ai)).

Potential Playbook Contributions:
- LinkedIn-as-PR operating model: treat executive profiles as media channels, not "personal branding exercises"
- Executive activation roster: founder + CMO + VP Sales + Head of CS posting cadence template
- Community-as-content-engine: mine member conversations for observation posts and podcast topics
- Guest sourcing via LinkedIn CTAs (comment-to-pitch format for owned media pipelines)
- Brand decoupling playbook: when and how to evolve from founder-named brand to category brand
- Event-led community monetization: long-lead BTS content promoting IRL experiences
- Multi-account omnipresence framework for SaaS marketing teams
- AI content stance: share observations and demand proof-of-results over hype posts
- Self-reported attribution prompts to measure dark-social LinkedIn impact ("How did you hear about us?")
- Writing-first content mix (text posts outperform heavy creative for many B2B use cases)
