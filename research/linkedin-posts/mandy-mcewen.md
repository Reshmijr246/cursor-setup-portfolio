## Post 2

**Date:** June 10, 2026

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mandymcewen_outbound-isnt-deadyet-but-people-are-activity-7470484639871188992-eLIR

**Summary:**  
McEwen argues that outbound is not dead, but spreadsheet-style outreach is. She calls out cold email blasts, generic LinkedIn DMs, and fake personalization as symptoms of a broken outbound motion. Her recommended replacement is a trust-first system built on networking, brand-building, authority, visible expertise, credible profiles, useful content, and engagement before pitching. The post connects to a *Your Future Biz* episode about why outbound still works when teams stop blasting and start building trust first.

**Key Takeaway:**  
Cold outreach performs better when prospects have already seen the rep or executive demonstrate credibility. For B2B SaaS teams, LinkedIn organic content should warm the market before the DM, not act as a disconnected awareness channel.

## Post 3

**Date:** June 8, 2026

**Post URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mandymcewen_this-is-how-sales-teams-get-an-1187-reply-activity-7469772732180709376-hb44

**Summary:**  
McEwen highlights a social selling statistic: a LinkedIn DM plus profile visit combination produced an 11.87% reply rate from cold outreach, outperforming cold email and cold calling in the example she cites. She explains the logic behind the result: buyers are more likely to respond when they recognize the person contacting them. The post reframes LinkedIn as the trust layer around outbound, before and after emails, calls, and meetings. She lists the operating components of a social selling system: buyer-facing profiles, strategic profile views, relevant comments, useful content, human DMs, Sales Navigator workflows, and stronger follow-up.

**Key Takeaway:**  
LinkedIn should make every cold touch warmer. Revenue teams need an integrated social selling workflow where profile optimization, visibility, comments, content, DMs, and Sales Navigator support pipeline creation rather than existing as isolated "LinkedIn activity."

## Research Notes

### Recurring Themes
- Trust before the pitch
- LinkedIn as a revenue layer around outbound
- Buyer-facing profiles as conversion infrastructure
- Relationship-building through content and engagement
- Social selling systems over random activity
- Human DMs and strategic profile visits
- Executive visibility and authority building
- Sales Navigator integrated with content and outreach

### Potential Playbook Contributions
- Trust-before-DM outbound model: publish useful content, engage with target accounts, trigger profile visits, then send human DMs
- Buyer-facing profile audit: replace resume-style profiles with buyer problem, proof, credibility, and clear next-step positioning
- Warm outbound workflow: profile visit plus relevant comment plus personalized DM plus Sales Navigator tracking
- Social selling readiness checklist for sales teams: profile, content lane, comment habits, DM quality, follow-up language, Sales Navigator process
- Executive brand warm-up system: make decision-makers recognize leadership before sales outreach lands
- LinkedIn Live relationship loop: turn podcast recordings, customer conversations, or expert discussions into recurring live sessions
- Anti-spam outreach rules: no generic connection notes, no fake personalization, no pitch before context
- Pipeline attribution lens: evaluate LinkedIn by reply rates, meeting creation, warmed accounts, and pipeline influence rather than post likes alone
- Sales-team enablement template: train reps to use LinkedIn before email, after email, before calls, and after meetings
- Content-to-conversation CTA model: use posts to invite DMs, podcast listens, live attendance, and workflow consultations
