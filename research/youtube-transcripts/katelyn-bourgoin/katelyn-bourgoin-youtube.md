### Ownable Idea Framework

**Purpose:**

Help a founder or expert business create a message that is clearly differentiated and easy for buyers to remember.

**Steps:**

1. Identify the problem you solve that matters most to your audience.
2. Test how buyers currently talk about that problem.
3. Shape a simple, repeatable narrative around the insight.
4. Use that narrative consistently across content and conversations.

**Expected Outcome:**

A clearer market position and more recognizable LinkedIn content [web:64].

### Buyer Psychology Messaging Loop

**Purpose:**

Turn customer research into messaging that is more likely to convert.

**Steps:**

1. Gather research through interviews and conversations.
2. Extract language, fears, and goals from buyers.
3. Rewrite content and offers in that language.
4. Repeat the loop as the market changes.

**Expected Outcome:**

Content and messaging that feel more relevant and persuasive [web:64].

### Sticky Narrative System

**Purpose:**

Create a repeatable story that buyers can understand quickly and share with others.

**Steps:**

1. Distill the core message into a short narrative.
2. Make the idea easy to repeat in a post, comment, or sales conversation.
3. Reinforce it across LinkedIn content and external appearances.
4. Keep the language simple and memorable.

**Expected Outcome:**

Better recall, stronger word-of-mouth, and more consistent brand positioning [web:64].
