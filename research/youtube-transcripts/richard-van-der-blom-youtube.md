## Potential Playbook Contributions

### Authority-First LinkedIn Growth Framework

**Purpose:**

Build LinkedIn visibility around credibility and trust instead of chasing isolated viral posts.

**Steps:**

1. Define the authority territory the person or company wants to own.
2. Publish content that demonstrates expertise, judgment, and buyer understanding.
3. Use comments and collaborations to reinforce credibility in relevant conversations.
4. Measure saves, qualified engagement, profile visits, and business conversations.

**Expected Outcome:**

More durable audience trust and stronger relevance with buyers, partners, and industry peers.

**Applicable To:**

* Founders
* Executives
* Marketing Teams
* Sales Teams
* Employee Advocacy Programs

### Signal Quality Measurement Framework

**Purpose:**

Separate business-relevant LinkedIn performance from vanity metrics.

**Steps:**

1. Track visible reach and engagement, but do not treat them as final outcomes.
2. Add quality signals such as saves, thoughtful comments, target-account engagement, and profile visits.
3. Capture silent-buyer indicators such as inbound messages, meeting mentions, and sales conversations influenced by LinkedIn.
4. Review whether content supports authority, trust, and conversion signals over time.

**Expected Outcome:**

A measurement system that reflects pipeline influence and buyer trust more accurately than impressions alone.

**Applicable To:**

* Founders
* Executives
* Marketing Teams
* Sales Teams
* Employee Advocacy Programs

---

### Networked Distribution Framework

**Purpose:**

Use alliances, smart engagement, and collaboration to expand organic reach into relevant audiences.

**Steps:**

1. Identify high-relevance creators, executives, customers, partners, and category voices.
2. Engage with substantive comments before seeking collaboration.
3. Co-create or participate in discussions that expose the company to adjacent buyer audiences.
4. Repurpose media and podcast appearances into LinkedIn content that reinforces authority.

**Expected Outcome:**

Organic distribution that compounds through credible networks rather than relying only on the company's own audience.

**Applicable To:**
