## Potential Playbook Contributions

### Hook-Rehook Method

**Purpose:**

Increase the odds that a LinkedIn post gets read beyond the first lines.

**Steps:**

1. Write a specific opening line.
2. Use the second line to deepen curiosity or clarify stakes.
3. Keep the opening concise and mobile-friendly.
4. Avoid generic intros that waste the first impression.

**Expected Outcome:**

Higher “see more” clicks and stronger post retention [web:40].

### Monetizable Expertise Filter

**Purpose:**

Identify content topics that can attract attention and commercial interest.

**Steps:**

1. List the knowledge that improves a buyer’s outcome.
2. Turn those ideas into educational posts.
3. Add social proof or a concrete example.
4. Lead with why the insight matters.

**Expected Outcome:**

Content that builds trust and attracts more relevant audience attention [web:40].

**Purpose:**

Create a sustainable weekly posting rhythm for long-term LinkedIn growth.

**Steps:**

1. Publish four times per week.
2. Maintain consistent quality.
3. Mix educational and authority-driven content.
4. Repeat the cadence over time.

**Expected Outcome:**

Steady audience growth without relying on virality or burnout [web:40].
